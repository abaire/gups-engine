adjust_standing(holder, subject, amount) since api_version 1 feature: standing

Adjusts standing directly for holder and subject by amount.

ParameterPassedDefaultDoc
holder feature: standingpositional or namedMob view or faction label holding the opinion.
subject feature: standingpositional or namedActor view or faction label the opinion is about.
amount feature: standingpositional or namedInteger amount to adjust.

Example

mob("guard", name = "Guard")

def _adjust(ctx):
    guard = ctx.here("guard")
    ctx.adjust_standing(guard, ctx.actor, 15)
    ctx.send(ctx.actor, "standing=%d" % ctx.standing(guard, ctx.actor))

room("post", name = "Post", desc = "Post.", flags = ["dead_end_ok"],
     commands = {"adjust": _adjust})

Test

w = t.world(seed = 1)

for d in w.diagnostics():
    t.fail("%s" % d.message)

after(pulses, fn, **args) since api_version 1

Calls fn(ctx, **args) on this hook's owner after some pulses. fn must be a top-level function; args must be storable.

ParameterPassedDefaultDoc
pulsespositional or namedPulses to wait, at least 1.
fnpositional or namedTop-level function fn(ctx, **args) to call.
**argsextra namedKeyword arguments passed to fn; values must be storable (int, str, bool, list or dict).
