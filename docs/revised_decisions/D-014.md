## Commands (§4.9, §5.2)

### D-014 Rate limiting applies to fast-lane commands from sessions only — `rejected`
Needs revision:
Adopt a dual-limiter architecture:
1. Retain the per-session token bucket (capacity 20, refill 5 tokens/s) gating fast-lane command
   executions as specified in SPEC §5.2.
2. Introduce a transport-level or session-level raw line-rate limiter across all incoming input lines
   (including unknown verbs, tick-lane commands, and pre-login lines) to protect against DoS flood attacks.
   If raw line arrival exceeds a coarse threshold (e.g. 25 lines/sec), lines are dropped and abusive
   connections are disconnected.
*Code:* `World.handle(ClientInputEvent)`, `TokenBucket`, `Sessions.kt`.
