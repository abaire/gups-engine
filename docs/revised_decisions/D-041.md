## Combat (§5.4)

### D-041 Summon durations use an affect — `rejected`
Needs revision (Alternative 1):
1. Affects must support a lifecycle callback or trigger invoked when removed or expired. The trigger
   must explicitly state the reason why it was called (e.g. `EXPIRED` vs `REMOVED` / dispelled). Expiration
   trumps removal (if an affect reaches its natural expiration pulse, the trigger fires with reason `EXPIRED`).
2. The summon affect must banish the summoned mob (fades away, disengages combatants, and leaves the world)
   on both natural expiration and removal (e.g. when dispelled).
3. `Mob` must natively track its summoner (`summonedBy: ActorId?`) at spawn time so that both timed summons
   and permanent summons (`duration=None`) record their master/summoner for ownership, AI aggro, and minion limits.
4. In `World.processDeath`, death of a summoned mob (`victim.summonedBy != null`) must suppress XP awards to the killer
   and skip rolling prototype loot (`victim.proto.loot`), preventing infinite XP and loot farming.
*Code:* `World.applyEffect`, `World.expireAffect`, `World.removeAffect`, `Affect`, `Combat.kt`, `World.processDeath`.
