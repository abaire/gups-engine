## Commands (§4.9, §5.2)

### D-019 Commands not in M1 — `rejected`
Needs revision:
1. Add `unwield` as a supported command alias for `remove` (or via configurable aliases per D-013)
   so players are not met with "Huh?" when trying to unequip weapons.
2. Correct the phrasing regarding equipment slot handling: `remove` (and `unwield`) handles all equipment
   slots (both worn armor and wielded weapons), whereas `wield` is restricted exclusively to items with
   weapon wear slot (`wear = "wield"`).
3. Deferrals of commands to later milestones (`give`, `put`, `wear`, `use`, `quests`, and `@` tools) are
   informational changelog notes; their substantive implementations are governed by D-114, D-126, D-143,
   and D-144.
