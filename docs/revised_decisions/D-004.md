## Scheduler and initiative tiers (§5.1, §5.4.1)

### D-004 Stunned actors are skipped entirely — `rejected`
Needs revision: stun should interrupt pending actions and remove the active waitState during
the stun. When the stun resolves, the original action must not trigger, and the waitState
needs to be reapplied. The current implementation merely skips the actor entirely during
`runTiers`, which leaves casting paused and freezes waitState countdown in place.
*Code:* `World.runTiers`, `TierSnapshot.canAct`, `World.applyAffect`, `World.expireAffect`.
