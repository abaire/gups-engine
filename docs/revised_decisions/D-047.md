## Event dispatch and hooks (§4.4, §5.6)

### D-047 Self-only hooks — `rejected`
Needs revision (Alternative 1):
1. Retain the self-only dispatch architecture: `on_tick`, `on_combat_tick`, `on_spawn`, `on_tell`, `on_load`, `on_reset`, `on_wake`, and `on_nearby` fire directly on their owner via `invokeOn`, bypassing room observer dispatch.
2. For `on_combat_tick`, firing on the owner must include both the engaged actor and all equipped items worn/wielded by that actor that declare `on_combat_tick`, sorted by ascending `EntityId` (as mandated by SPEC §4.4: `Mob, Item (equipped)`).
3. In `World.runCombatRound`, after invoking `on_combat_tick` on the actor, iterate `a.equipmentIndex.values` and invoke `invokeOn(item, HookName.ON_COMBAT_TICK, HookContext(actor = a, room = a.room))` on each equipped item having the hook, with `ctx.self = item` and `ctx.targets = opponents`.
4. `on_tick` continues to fire on every simulated room, actor, and item (SPEC §5.9) that has the hook, sorted by ascending `EntityId`.
*Code:* `World.invokeOn`, `World.runCombatRound`, `World.runWorldTick`, `Hooks.kt`.
