## Combat (§5.4)

### D-037 Spell damage goes through `mitigate`; only damage starts combat — `rejected`
Needs revision (Alternative 1):
1. The rule that direct spell damage rolled from `Effect.Damage` is routed through `rules.mitigate` is ratified.
2. However, "a hostile skill landing" (§5.4) must not be restricted strictly to direct `Effect.Damage`.
   Restricting combat initiation to damage allows exploits where players can repeatedly stun, silence, debuff,
   knock back, or pull enemy mobs without aggroing them or entering combat.
3. Revise `CastAction.resolve` and `World.applyEffect` so that combat begins (`startCombat(caster, target)`) whenever:
   - A skill declared with `skill.target == TargetKind.ENEMY` (or room-wide offensive skills) lands on an actor
     other than the caster; or
   - An effect primitive dealing `Effect.Damage`, `Effect.Stun`, or displacement (`Effect.Knockback` / `Effect.Pull`)
     lands on an actor other than the caster.
*Code:* `World.applyEffect`, `CastAction.resolve`.
