## Combat (§5.4)

### D-035 `before_attack` dispatches on the defender — `rejected`
Needs revision (Alternative 1):
1. While the defender remains the primary entity in `before_attack` dispatch so that defensive wards, shields,
   and sanctuary effects evaluate first, the dispatch chain must not exclude the attacker (`ctx.actor`) or their
   equipment.
2. Revise `World.dispatch` / `World.autoAttack` so that the dispatch order for `before_attack` includes:
   the defender (`primary`), followed by the attacker (`ctx.actor`), the room, other observers in the room,
   room items, and equipped/carried items of the defender, the attacker (specifically including `ctx.weapon` /
   equipped weapon), and observers.
3. This resolves the code bug where `World.dispatch` excluded `ctx.actor` and completely omitted the attacker's
   equipped weapon from receiving `before_attack`, ensuring cursed weapons, durability/ammo restrictions,
   and attacker status effects function properly.
*Code:* `World.autoAttack`, `World.dispatch`.
