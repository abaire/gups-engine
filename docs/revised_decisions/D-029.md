## Tactical track (§5.5)

### D-029 Flee details — `rejected`
Needs revision:
1. Rename `blocks` on exits, doors, and related models to `senses_blocked` across Starlark schemas
   and Kotlin models (e.g. `senses_blocked = ALL` or `senses_blocked = ["sight", "sound"]`) to
   completely eliminate ambiguity between sensory perception propagation and physical movement passability.
2. Fleeing through an exit must trigger `before_exit` and `before_enter` hooks (including guard denial
   checks) rather than bypassing them. This prevents the exploit where players initiate combat (with low-level
   mobs or in PvP) specifically to use `flee` to bypass guarded or restricted doorways.
*Code:* `FleeAction`, `Exit.passable`, `Declarations.exit`, `Movement.kt`.
