## Event dispatch and hooks (§4.4, §5.6)

### D-046 Who counts as an observer — `rejected`
Needs revision (Alternative 1):
1. Revise `World.dispatch` so that the dispatch order includes all participants and their items:
   - Primary Entity: `primary` (if non-null and distinct from `ctx.actor`, e.g. defender in attacks, item being manipulated).
   - Acting Actor: `ctx.actor` (if non-null and not already `primary`).
   - Room: `room` (`ctx.room` / event room).
   - Bystanders: Other actors in the room (excluding `primary` and `ctx.actor`), sorted by ascending `EntityId`.
   - Ground Items: Items on the ground in the room, sorted by ascending `EntityId`.
   - Carried & Equipped Items: All items carried or equipped by `primary` (if an Actor), the acting actor (`ctx.actor`), and bystander actors, sorted by ascending `EntityId`.
2. All entity owners are de-duplicated preserving first-seen order via `LinkedHashSet<Entity>()`.
3. This resolves the critical architectural flaw where `ctx.actor`'s carried and equipped items were completely excluded from `holders`, ensuring that weapons, worn gear (cursed items, stat equipment), and inventory items of both the actor and the target observe and participate in event dispatch.
*Code:* `World.dispatch`, `Hooks.kt`.
