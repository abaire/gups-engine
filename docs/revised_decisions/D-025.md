## Action lifecycle (§5.3)

### D-025 Cast messages — `rejected`
Needs revision:
1. In `CastAction.resolve`, add an echo to room bystanders (excluding the caster and the target):
   `"<Name> casts <skill> [at <target> / on <item>]."` so that onlookers and group members perceive
   when a spell completes, and instant spells (`warmup == 0`) are not completely invisible to the room.
2. In `CastAction.begin` (warmup phase), onlookers who know the same spell (`viewer.skills.containsKey(skill.label)`)
   must be able to recognize it, seeing `"<Name> begins casting <skill>."`, while onlookers who do not know the spell
   continue to see the generic `"<Name> begins casting."`.
   (Note for future enhancement: allow characters with high Wisdom/Intelligence or perception rules checks to
   recognize unfamiliar spells by observing the caster's actions).
*Code:* `CastAction.begin`, `CastAction.resolve`.
