## Tactical track (§5.5)

### D-031 Doors are synchronized on both sides — `rejected`
Needs revision:
While two-way door synchronization and reverse-room echoes are accepted:
1. Lock and unlock key checks for doors and containers must not be restricted strictly to carried inventory;
   they must inspect equipped items as well (e.g. keys held in hand or worn on a chain).
2. Introduce a built-in automatic "keyring" on characters. Key items that have no purpose other than acting as
   a key are automatically stored on the keyring (preventing inventory clutter), and keys on the keyring are
   automatically recognized and counted when locking or unlocking doors and containers.
*Code:* `EngineCommands.planDoor`, `EngineCommands.planContainer`, `World.reverseExit`, `World.setDoor`.
