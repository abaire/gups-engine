rules_ctx since api_version 1

The read-only context of a rules call or modifier.

Fields

FieldSinceDoc
action1For door_intensity: 'open', 'close', 'lock' or 'unlock'.
actor1Whom the call is about: the attacker, caster, mover or regenerating actor.
affect1In a modifier contributed by an actor affect or an item instance affect, that affect's view; None everywhere else.
attacker1For attack calls, the same as actor.
band feature: track1'melee', 'near' or 'far' between actor and target, or None.
carried1The weight carried and worn, containers' contents included; None outside encumbrance_level and encumbrance.
channel1For the perception calls: the channel name, such as 'sound'.
contributors1The victim's contributors, as contributor structs, in xp_share; None elsewhere.
cost1For can_perceive and perceive_message: the attenuation summed along the cheapest path from the event's room; 0 in that room.
damage_type1The damage type as a string in auto-attack to_hit and damage calls, damage chains and mitigate, otherwise None.
defender1For attack calls, the one attacked; also the one taking damage in mitigate.
encumbrance_level1The level encumbrance_level just gave; None outside encumbrance.
event1For can_perceive and perceive_message: the event name, such as 'say' or 'combat'.
exit1For sense_cost: the exit of ctx.room leading back toward the signal's source. For door_intensity: the door's exit, or None for a container.
intensity1For can_perceive and perceive_message: the event's strength where it happened.
item1The item, for get_lag and carry_limit.
limit1The actor's carry limit asked without an item; None outside encumbrance_level and encumbrance.
pulse1Game time; not available in xp_to_level.
rng1The seeded random number generator; not available in xp_to_level.
room1The actor's room; for sense_cost, the room the signal enters.
skill1The skill being cast or evaluated, or None.
slot1The slot of the swinging item in an auto-attack swing, 'wield' for an unarmed swing, otherwise None.
source1For can_perceive and perceive_message: the actor who caused the event, or None.
source_room1For can_perceive and perceive_message: the room the event happened in.
tags1For door_intensity: the door's or container's tags, sorted.
target1The other actor of the call, if any: the defender, the pinner, the victim, the killer, or the actor advanced on or retreated from.
text1For can_perceive and perceive_message: the event's text, or None.
via_exit1For can_perceive and perceive_message: the listener's exit leading toward the event, or None in the event's own room.
weapon1The weapon used in an attack, or None for unarmed.
xp1The experience pool a kill is being settled for, in xp_share; None elsewhere.

Adjusts the recovery lag in pulses after advance. ctx.target is the actor approached; ctx.band is the band after the step. The final result is clamped to 0.
