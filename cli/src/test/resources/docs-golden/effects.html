Effects and behaviors

Effect primitives

Declarative effects for skill(effect=...).

heal(dice, vital) since api_version 1 feature: skills

Restore a vital, up to its maximum.

ParameterPassedDefaultDoc
dice feature: skillspositional or namedAmount restored, as dice such as '2d4'.
vital feature: skillspositional or namedThe declared vital to restore.

Example

skill("mend", kind = "spell", cost = vitals(mana = 5), target = "ally",
      effect = heal(dice = "2d4", vital = "hp"))

Behaviors

Built-in mob behaviors for mob(behaviors=[...]).

wander(radius = 3, chance = 0.1) since api_version 1

Behavior: with probability chance per think, steps through a random open exit within radius exits of home.

ParameterPassedDefaultDoc
radiuspositional or named3Maximum distance from home, in exits.
chancepositional or named0.1Probability of taking a step on each think, between 0 and 1.
